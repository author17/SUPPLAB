Add SUPPLAB to Your Home Screen

Get the app-like experience on iPhone and iPad — full screen, faster loading, and one tap from your home screen. Apple requires this to be done manually in Safari.

  1. 1 Open supplab.com in Safari (this only works in Safari — not Chrome or another browser on iOS).
  2. 2 Tap the ⬆️ Share button in the toolbar (on iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen; on iPad it's at the top).
  3. 3 Scroll down the share menu and tap ➕ Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4 Confirm the name (SUPPLAB is filled in automatically) and tap Add in the top right.
  5. 5 That's it — a SUPPLAB icon now sits on your home screen. Open it any time for the full-screen app experience.
Why can't we just show an "Install" button? Apple's Safari doesn't allow websites to trigger the Add to Home Screen prompt automatically the way Android/Chrome does — it has to be started by you, from the Share menu, every time. This page just walks you through Apple's own steps.

Using Chrome or another browser on iPhone? Open this page in Safari first — only Safari can add SUPPLAB to your home screen.
